## Sign-in

Chief Tools supports several ways to sign in and secure your account.

### Username and password

Your Chief Tools username is your email address. Change it from your <ExternalLink href="https://account.chief.app/settings/profile">profile</ExternalLink>, or update your password from the <ExternalLink href="https://account.chief.app/settings/password">password settings</ExternalLink> page.

### Two-factor

You can enable a time-based one-time password in an authenticator such as 1Password or Google Authenticator. Account Chief asks for a generated code when you sign in.

### Passkeys

Register a passkey to use it as a second factor or to sign in without entering your email address and password.

### Social login

You can <ExternalLink href="https://account.chief.app/account/social">connect a social account</ExternalLink> and sign in with GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, or Apple.

If the connected account already uses strong authentication, your <ExternalLink href="https://account.chief.app/account/preferences">preferences</ExternalLink> can allow that social sign-in to bypass Account Chief's second factor.
