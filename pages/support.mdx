Start with the documentation for how-to help. Use the roadmap for feature requests, or contact us when you need an answer from the team.

## Find an answer

Use **Search** at the top of any documentation page to look across all Chief Tools products. You can also [choose a product](/introduction#choose-a-product) and browse its guides.

If a guide is unclear or incomplete, use **Edit this page** at the bottom to propose a correction on GitHub.

## Suggest or follow a feature

Our <ExternalLink href="https://roadmap.chief.tools">public roadmap</ExternalLink> shows planned and requested work. Sign in with your Chief Tools account to vote or submit an idea.

## Contact us

Use the <ExternalLink href="https://chief.app/contact">contact form</ExternalLink> if the documentation does not answer your question, you need help with your account, or the matter is private.

Include enough detail for us to find the problem:

- the Chief Tools product and team involved;
- what you were trying to do;
- what happened and what you expected instead;
- the approximate time of the problem, including your time zone;
- the full error message and a screenshot, if available.

Do not send passwords, access tokens, authorization codes, private keys, or complete payment-card details.

<Callout type="caution" title="Report security issues privately">
  If you found a vulnerability, follow the [security disclosure policy](/security#security-disclosure-policy) and email security@chief.app. Do not post vulnerability details on the public roadmap or in a public issue.
</Callout>
